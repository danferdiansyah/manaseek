# Home service icons

Eight separate 3D icons generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool for the
home service grid. The final assets are 192 × 192 WebP files, encoded at
quality 88 with their original alpha channels preserved. CSS supplies the
frosted glass tile; the illustrations contain no baked-in tile or backdrop.

## Final prompt set

Each icon used the shared style prompt below followed by its own subject
description. Each was generated in a separate built-in tool call.

### Shared style

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated 3D menu icon for the Manaseek Hajj and Umrah mobile app. Style: premium soft 3D product render with rounded beveled edges, deep emerald enamel, subtle frosted jade-glass details and satin gold accents; dimensional, tactile, restrained, no cartoon face. Palette: emerald #146144, forest green #064b33, mint #adcbb5, warm gold #d7b269, ivory only for small supporting details. Camera: consistent orthographic three-quarter view, facing slightly left, mild 15-degree top-down elevation. Lighting: soft studio light from upper left, clean highlights, readable shadows on the object itself. Composition: one large cohesive icon centered in a square, occupies about 80 percent of canvas, 8 percent clear padding, all parts visible. Make the silhouette bold and recognizable at 56px. Background: genuine transparent RGBA; no backdrop, no floor, no ground shadow, no tile, no circular background, no pedestal unless specified. No words, no lettering, no calligraphy, no watermark, no interface, no icon sheet.

### Panduan Umrah

> Subject: a simplified three-dimensional Kaaba cube, matte charcoal-black kiswah, a broad satin gold band near the top and one small raised gold door. Use large clean shapes without inscriptions or ornamental patterns. The complete Kaaba only, no mosque buildings or scenery.

### Panduan Haji

> Subject: a thick open Hajj guidebook, emerald green rounded hardcover, two clearly sculpted ivory page blocks, a satin gold bookmark ribbon falling down the center. A small raised geometric gold Kaaba symbol appears on one page. No writing or lines of text. The book only, with its open pages visible.

### Tanya AI

> Subject: a rounded emerald green three-dimensional speech bubble with a short tail, three raised satin-gold dots across its front, and a smaller pale jade translucent speech bubble partly behind it. The pair forms one compact chat-assistant icon, no extra sparkles or text.

### Cari Mutawif

> Subject: a friendly stylized faceless male pilgrimage guide bust, warm ivory featureless head, small emerald kufi cap, deep emerald shoulders and tunic. A small raised satin-gold checkmark badge nestles at the lower-right shoulder to signify a guide. No eyes, no mouth, no realistic portrait. Simple icon-like proportions, head and shoulders only.

### Arah Kiblat

> Subject: a chunky circular navigation compass in three-quarter view, deep emerald body, satin gold rim, translucent jade glass face and a prominent raised two-tone gold-and-emerald directional needle. Only four tiny tick marks, no letters, no numbers, no maps. The compass is the whole icon.

### Checklist Ibadah

> Subject: a softly beveled emerald clipboard with a satin-gold clip on top, an ivory inset sheet, and three large raised green checkmarks beside three short sculpted green horizontal bars. Keep the marks large and sparse; no letters or numbers. The clipboard is the whole icon.

### Persiapan Perjalanan

> Subject: a compact emerald green rolling suitcase with softly rounded edges, shallow vertical ridges, a satin-gold telescoping handle and two small dark green wheels. Add a small gold luggage tag on its side. One suitcase only, no plane, no passport, no scenery.

### Profil Saya

> Subject: a freestanding generic account avatar sculpted in emerald green: a smooth spherical head floating just above simple rounded shoulders and torso, with a small satin-gold collar detail. Featureless universal user silhouette, no face, no hair, no hat, no frame or badge. Clearly distinct from a dressed pilgrimage guide.
