# Prayer card 3D icons

Seven individual assets generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for `lib/PrayerStrip.jsx`. The matte clay style matches the existing Manaseek icon family. Each image is optimized to 192 × 192 WebP, preserving the generated transparent alpha channel (quality 88, alpha quality 100).

The featured next-prayer illustration reuses the corresponding prayer icon. The compass reuses the existing [3D qibla artwork](../services/qibla.webp).

## Final prompt set

Each asset was generated in a separate built-in tool call using the shared style followed by its subject below.

### Shared style

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one 3D UI icon for the prayer schedule card in Manaseek, a calm Hajj and Umrah mobile app. Style: minimalist, softly sculpted SOLID MATTE clay, fully opaque objects with gentle rounded bevels and clearly visible dimensional depth. Match a family of forest-green, sage, ivory and subdued ochre clay icons. Palette: forest green #3D6852, pale sage #B9C9B9, warm ivory #F1EFE6, warm muted ochre #CBA25C. Orthographic subtle three-quarter view, 12-degree view from above, front and a little right side visible. Broad soft studio light from upper left, gentle ambient occlusion, restrained contrast. Single centered icon fills 80 percent of a square canvas with even clear padding, readable at 32px and attractive at 80px. Genuine transparent RGBA background, isolated object cutout. No text, letters, numerals, faces, calligraphy, logos, watermarks, border, badge, enclosing tile, floor, platform, backdrop, ground shadow, shiny metal, glass, plastic gloss, dramatic highlights, ornate details, floating decorative particles or icon sheet. Generate exactly one icon.

### subuh

Subject: dawn, represented by one pale warm-ochre half sun emerging above a single short thick horizontal pale-sage horizon bar. Exactly three short chunky rounded ivory rays around the upper semicircle. The sun is light cream-gold and the horizon is cool sage, for a quiet early-morning feeling. Compact simple silhouette. No arrows, no cloud.

### zuhur

Subject: the midday sun, one full warm muted-ochre circular sun disk with exactly eight short thick rounded ochre rays evenly spaced around it. A simple solid clay sun, fuller and warmer than the dawn icon. No clouds, no horizon, no arrows.

### asar

Subject: late-afternoon daylight, one warm muted-ochre sun disk with five short chunky rounded rays, partly behind one small puffy pale-sage cloud covering its lower-right quadrant. The cloud uses only three smooth rounded lobes. Sun remains dominant and clearly identifiable. No horizon, no arrows.

### magrib

Subject: sunset, one warm muted terracotta-orange #C78D5D half sun with three short thick rounded rays, sinking behind one broad low forest-green #3D6852 horizontal horizon bar. The sun is richly warm and the horizon dark green, visibly distinct from the pale sage dawn icon. Clean compact half-sun silhouette. No arrow, no cloud.

### isya

Subject: night, one thick upright crescent moon made from warm ivory clay with pale-sage shaded sides, opening to the upper right. One small simple muted-ochre four-point star nestled beside the crescent's opening. Rounded moon tips and rounded star tips. Strong clean silhouette, no face, no extra stars.

### location

Subject: one upright forest-green teardrop-shaped map location pin, with softly rounded edges and an inset round warm-ivory center. A simple substantial 3D pin, not a thin outline. No map, no base, no rings, no extra objects.

### chevron

Subject: one single right-pointing chevron, shaped like >, made from thick forest-green matte clay. Two short rounded beams joined at a gently rounded point, subtle extrusion depth, clean simple silhouette. The chevron occupies 60 percent of canvas width and 80 percent of height. No arrow shaft, no circular enclosure, no extra objects.
